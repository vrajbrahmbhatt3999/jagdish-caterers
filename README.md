# Jagdish Caterers

> India's Premier Luxury Wedding Catering House & Bespoke Culinary Theatres

A luxury hospitality web portal showcasing Jagdish Caterers' regal culinary legacy, signature verticals, interactive live gastronomy theatres, curated visual anthology, patron reflections, and VIP consultation booking.

## Features

- **Cinematic Video Hero**: Multi-reel drone aerials and banquet cinematography with interactive reel selection.
- **The House & Legacy**: Over three decades of royal culinary heritage, pedigree statistics, and heirloom court recipes.
- **Signature Verticals**: Luxe Weddings, Pan-India Destinations, Privé Conclaves, and Grand Arenas.
- **Interactive Culinary Theatres**: Dynamic live gastronomy pavilions (Royal Dastarkhwan, Satvik & Pure Jain, Artisanal European, Pan-Asian Robata, Grand Patisserie).
- **The Atelier**: Four-stage bespoke curation journey from Palate Mapping to Event Symphony.
- **Visual Anthology**: Filterable photo gallery with high-resolution lightbox modal.
- **Patron Acclaim**: Video testimonials with custom player modal and press recognition.
- **VIP Concierge & Event Planner**: Interactive event inquiry system with direct WhatsApp VIP integration.

## Technologies

- HTML5 (Semantic & Accessible)
- Vanilla CSS3 (Custom Luxury Design System)
- Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+ interactive engine)
