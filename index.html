THE HOUSE OF JAGDISH

Masters of Royal Indian Gastronomy for Palace Nuptials & Grand Conclaves.

For more than three decades, Jagdish Caterers has stood as India’s foremost authority in regal hospitality. Rooted in authentic culinary traditions and royal court recipes, we curate extraordinary palace weddings, multi-day destination celebrations, and grand industrialist banquets across India.

From the magnificent lake palaces of Udaipur to the royal courtyards of Jaipur and Jodhpur, the historic estates of Gujarat, and the iconic grand ballrooms of Mumbai and Delhi NCR, we choreograph every feast as an unforgettable royal celebration. Our master bawarchis, regional culinary artisans, and white-glove banquet captains orchestrate authentic flavors, theatrical live tandoors, and warm Indian hospitality into a flawless symphony.

The Royal Awadhi & Dum Pukht Dastarkhwan

Echoing the regal kitchens of Lucknow and Hyderabad, this theatre features copper degs sealed with dough and slow-simmered over fragrant wood embers. Master bawarchis carve, garnish, and present dishes on bespoke engraved brass and silver plateware.
